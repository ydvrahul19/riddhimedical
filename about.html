About Riddhi Medical

Excellence in healthcare with a human touch since 2010

Healing with Compassion & Technology

Founded in 2010, Riddhi Medical & Clinic has grown from a small neighborhood clinic to a trusted multi-specialty healthcare center. Our journey has been guided by a simple philosophy: every patient deserves world-class medical care delivered with warmth and respect.

We believe that healthcare is not just about treating illnesses—it's about building lasting relationships with our patients and their families. Our state-of-the-art facilities combined with our experienced medical team ensure that you receive comprehensive care under one roof.
