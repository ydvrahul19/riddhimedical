✦ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a doctor's referral to visit Riddhi Medical? +
No referral is needed. You can visit us directly or WhatsApp us to book an appointment. However, if you have reports or scans from a previous doctor, please bring them as they help us plan your treatment better.
How many sessions of physiotherapy will I need? +
The number of sessions depends on your condition, its severity, and your response to treatment. After a thorough initial assessment, we will give you a realistic estimate. Most acute conditions improve within 6–12 sessions; chronic or neurological conditions may require longer programmes.
Do you offer home visit physiotherapy? +
Yes, we do offer home visit physiotherapy for patients who are unable to travel due to post-surgical recovery, severe mobility limitations, or other health conditions. Please call or WhatsApp +91 94625 91098 to arrange a home visit and confirm availability.
Can physiotherapy help after a stroke or neurological condition? +
Absolutely. Our team specialises in neurological physiotherapy for stroke rehabilitation, Parkinson's disease, spinal cord injury, and cerebral palsy. We use task-specific training and neuroplasticity principles to help patients regain movement, strength, and independence.
What should I wear to my physiotherapy session? +
Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothes that allow easy access to the affected area. For lower limb conditions, shorts are ideal. For shoulder or back conditions, a loose t-shirt is recommended. Comfortable footwear is also advised.
Is physiotherapy painful? +
Most physiotherapy techniques are not painful. You may experience some mild discomfort during certain manual therapy or exercise techniques, but our therapists always work within your pain threshold and will adjust any technique that causes excessive discomfort. Open communication with your therapist is always encouraged.
